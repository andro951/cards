# Choices to keep

- Static, free hosting and optional local preview meet the final infrastructure requirement.
- Keeping the established domain/compiler and pinned Card Conjurer avoids replacing proven card-layout logic unnecessarily.
- Orange styling, official logo, top navigation, small import modal and separate Choose Look match the requested presentation.
- Static frame placeholders and immediate selection remove unwanted generation during setup. Do not revert this to generated previews.
- Per-face art/printing choices, explicit credits, manual overrides, warning acknowledgements tied to renders, full-screen review and paired immutable exports are worthwhile additions.
- Classic/newer token families, Godzilla variants, numbered filename matching, and specialized card fixes address real functional needs.
- Named presets, optional folder storage and selective portable backups are useful when persistence and recovery are reliable.
- Separate old/new launchers retain a practical comparison and fallback during stabilization.
